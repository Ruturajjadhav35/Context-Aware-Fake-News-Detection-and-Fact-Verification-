How The Desk Works

Four stages, one wire

Every claim you submit above travels this same route, start to verdict.

STAGE 01 — EXTRACT
Claim extraction

spaCy’s dependency parser isolates the checkable factual assertion inside a longer passage, discarding opinion, hedged language, and rhetorical framing.

STAGE 02 — SCREEN
BERT classification

A BERT model fine‑tuned on the ISOT fake‑news corpus gives the claim a first‑pass credibility score — 92% accuracy on held‑out test data.

STAGE 03 — RETRIEVE
Evidence retrieval

The claim is queried against Google Fact Check Tools, NewsAPI, The Guardian Open Platform, and Wikipedia to pull independent, citable evidence.

STAGE 04 — VERIFY
RoBERTa‑MNLI verdict

A RoBERTa model fine‑tuned on MNLI checks each passage against the claim for entailment, contradiction, or neutrality; votes are weighted by source into one verdict.
